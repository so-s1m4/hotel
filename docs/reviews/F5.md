## What changed in weak-flow reasoning

The strong-room path remains a 60-minute candidate at 3–100 L/15 min, subject to
known vacancy/cleanliness, night, operations and meter-quality checks. Its inspection
proposals are separate from the new uncertain weak-flow review.

The earlier low-rate path remains available: at least three hours of stable
1..<3 L/15 min plus an independent quiet baseline from earlier local nights. A
missing or already elevated baseline prevents that comparison. The new path does
not reinterpret that absence as proof of a leak, or elevated history as proof that
persistent consumption is normal.

Instead, `PersistentWeakFlowDetector` can flag persistent unexplained flow after
four elapsed hours of causally eligible observations. The caller must establish
fresh observed vacancy and cleanliness at both endpoints and exclude known
operations and lifecycle changes. The helper independently checks exact 15-minute
raw pairs, finite nonnegative counters, continuity and local night boundaries.
There are no room IDs, scenario dates or corrective-work narratives in the rule.

The default review requires:

- Sixteen uninterrupted intervals with signed endpoint net averaging 1..<3 L/15 min.
- Meaningful positive progress in at least 75% of intervals and at least two in
  every hour; one interval cannot contribute over 20% of the complete net increase.
- Every hourly endpoint net at least 2 L, with largest/smallest hourly net ratio
  at most four. This admits moderate gradual change without accepting one sparse
  tap burst as continuous flow.
- At most two small reverse steps, each at most 0.5 L, with total reverse volume
  at most `min(1 L, 10% of signed net)`. Negative amounts are subtracted, never
  clipped to zero. Missing pairs, gaps, large resets and conflicting readings break
  evidence rather than being interpolated.

These limits are documented prototype assumptions, not calibrated meter accuracy.
Sparse hourly taps, coarse quantization, zero-net oscillation and heavy counter
jitter are excluded, so coverage remains conservative. Default 240 minutes fits
the unchanged `[00:00,06:00)` local-night gate, including spring DST. The optional
300-minute setting may not fit a short spring night; a silently unreachable normal
six-hour requirement is not offered.

Both low-rate paths emit only `flag_anomaly` under the same room episode/action
identity. Overlapping qualifying windows therefore do not become repeated flags.
An alert explicitly retains meter drift/bias, calibration, quantization and
unrecorded activity as alternatives. It does not authorize room entry, inspection,
shutoff, service or a claimed repair. The executor audit records signed
`weak_water_metrics`; `effects_applied` and `observed_history_changed` remain false.

## Availability, operation boundaries and continuing episodes

Known operation windows use inclusive endpoints. A pair whose start is still in
an operation does not become eligible merely because its end is fifteen minutes
past expiry. The strong-flow path checks every reading endpoint in its full hour.
Newly available information can also disqualify a previously retained interval:
availability at the current decision time is kept separate from the time covered
by the operation. A late-known scope invalidates affected weak-run and independent
baseline evidence, retaining an unaffected continuous tail rather than clearing
all valid later observations. Previously emitted historical decisions are not
rewritten using later facts.

Creation envelopes keep future narratives hidden. A scope or narrative revealed
by an observed completion can update current context only at that event's
availability time. The complete engine and paired evaluator group equal-time
facts before a periodic scan: lexical `tick:` before `wo:` must not hide an
already available completion or order from the scan. Each envelope is copied
before buffering, so a streaming adapter reusing a mutable object cannot replace
a previous event during lookahead. Future occurrence/source times and decreasing
availability are rejected. A standalone `process(event)` cannot know a same-time
fact its caller has not yet supplied; use `run()` for complete timestamp batches.

Strong flow that reduces to weak but continuing flow is not recovery. A room
with an active strong finding retains that episode and does not gain another
weaker review simply because consumption fell below the strong threshold. A
strong finding also supersedes a same-tick weak candidate for that room only.
Observed quiet recovery still permits a later independent weak incident; a gap,
invalid meter or assumed repair does not establish that recovery. Tight numeric
boundary guards preserve configured weak/strong bands instead of promoting a
weak midpoint to an inspection under unusually narrow custom thresholds.

Regressions cover inclusive expiry, late-known scopes, retained clean tails,
independent-baseline invalidation, equal-time order/completion arrival, future
completion noninterference, mutable envelope reuse, strong/weak transitions,
cross-room independence and observed recovery. They do not establish leak truth.

## Main balance: use available pairs without inventing missing rooms

Previously, one bad sample anywhere in the preceding hour blocked the latest
residual even when every current adjacent main/room pair was usable. The candidate
retains each genuinely complete latest interval in its baseline/history. It still
requires actual matching current pairs from all rooms and the main meter, and a
sustained main-balance signal still needs four consecutive complete intervals.

| Profile | Ticks evaluated | Computable latest residual under old hourly prerequisite | Computable latest residual under current-pair prerequisite | Additional usable ticks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Quickstart | 2,016 | 872 | 1,415 | 543 |
| Dev | 35,040 | 12,356 | 22,969 | 10,613 |

This is increased assessment availability, not a detected-leak or recall gain.
There are still 601 Quickstart and 12,071 Dev ticks without a complete current
sum. Missing/invalid rooms remain unknown: their readings are not zero-filled,
interpolated, reconstructed from future samples or silently replaced by a total
from a different interval. Exclusion counters overlap, so they must not be summed
as independent incident counts. The [completeness cross-check](f5_completeness.json)
and paired evaluation preserve the raw exposure diagnostics.

## Remaining limits and reproduction

Slow linear meter drift can exactly mimic low water flow. There is no statistical
confidence or leak truth in this comparison, and no incident-labelled denominator
for recall. Unobserved operations, occupancy uncertainty, sensor outages, coarse
quantization, large jitter and late-night onset can prevent review; occupied/daytime
weak flow remains outside these rules. A review flag needs independent meter and
fixture verification, not automatic intervention. The main-balance branch cannot
recover information that the current sensors did not provide.

Run the complete independent negative controls, then reproduce water diagnostics
and each full decisions/audit pair separately; do not concatenate the profiles:

```bash
python -m pytest -q tests/engine/test_weak_water.py tests/engine/test_weak_water_adversarial.py
python -m pytest -q
python -m scripts.evaluate_water --data-dir data/PLATO40_v2_quickstart --data-dir data/PLATO40_v2_dev --baseline-ref 3ab9541a02ce72ef8b3c70d27fdf2868e94dc6c4 --out outputs/f5_paired_evaluation.json
python run.py --data-dir data/PLATO40_v2_dev --out outputs/dev_decisions.jsonl --audit outputs/dev_audit.jsonl
python -m engine.validation --data-dir data/PLATO40_v2_dev --decisions outputs/dev_decisions.jsonl --audit outputs/dev_audit.jsonl --out outputs/dev_validation.json
```

The paired evaluator records the immutable baseline commit, candidate source hashes
